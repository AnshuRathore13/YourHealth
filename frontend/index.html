✦ Core Features

Everything a modern
healthcare platform needs

From intelligent booking to AI-powered follow-ups — built for patients, doctors, and administrators.

🧠
AI Pre-Visit Summaries
Patients submit symptoms before booking. Gemini AI generates urgency level (Low / Medium / High) and suggested questions for the doctor.
📋
Post-Visit AI Summaries
Doctors submit clinical notes, and AI transforms them into patient-friendly summaries with a clear medication schedule.
🔒
Double-Booking Prevention
Slot locking mechanism ensures no two patients can book the same slot simultaneously, with atomic DB transactions.
📅
Google Calendar Sync
Appointments automatically create/update/delete calendar events for both patients and doctors via Google Calendar OAuth.
💊
Smart Medication Reminders
Background worker sends email reminders based on prescription frequency — once, twice, or thrice daily.
🏥
Doctor Leave Management
When a doctor marks leave, all affected bookings are detected and patients are automatically notified to reschedule.
